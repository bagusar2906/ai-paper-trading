Self-training while the app is running

Uses these training settings and the selected market-data source. Saves completed candles and catches up from the last saved candle after a shutdown, before training. Downloads missed history in batches, skips unchanged training inputs, and creates candidates for review. Training uses the latest configured number of candles; older downloaded candles remain saved. Catch-up depends on history available from your data source. Learning happens during retraining; it does not replace the champion automatically. Stopping prevents future runs; a run already in progress can finish.
